Stage transition rules: try them out

The question: Do the proposed pipeline rules (Option C) feel right? Can recruiters and hiring managers move an application where they need to, while the system blocks moves that make no sense?

  • Applied, Screening, Interview, and Final Review are active. From an active stage you can jump forward any number of steps, or go back exactly one step.
  • Final Review → Offer and Offer → Hired are Hiring Manager only.
  • Anyone can move an active or Offer application to Rejected or Withdrawn.
  • Hired, Rejected, and Withdrawn are closed outcomes. Only a Hiring Manager can reopen Rejected or Withdrawn, which returns the application to the stage it was in before. Hired is permanent.
